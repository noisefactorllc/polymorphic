Rainbow fringing around every edge, far more colourful than any real lens would
produce.

It starts like ordinary chromatic aberration, pulling the colour channels apart
by an amount that grows with distance from the centre. But instead of showing
you that, it measures how far apart they ended up and throws the colours away,
keeping only a single number for how strong the fringing is at each point.

The rainbow is then invented from scratch. That one number is stretched across
the entire colour wheel and given full saturation, so a narrow range of edge
strengths becomes every hue there is. This is false colour, the same technique
that paints heat maps and weather radar, applied to edge strength.

The original picture is drained of its own colour and added back underneath, so
you get a grey image wearing a rainbow outline. `passthru` sets how much of it
shows.
