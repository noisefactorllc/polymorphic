The colour smear a picture tube makes at the moment you press the degauss
button, red green and blue pulling apart into fringes before snapping back.

Each colour channel is dragged in its own direction, steered by a noise field,
so the three no longer sit on top of one another. Rather than affecting the
frame evenly, the distortion is concentrated around a point and ripples outward,
which is what the magnetic pulse in a real tube does.
