Two takes on breaking a picture into tiles.

Mosaic goes for the ceramic look. A square grid is warped with noise so the
tile edges wander instead of running dead straight, and each tile is flattened
to a single colour taken from somewhere within itself. The grout between tiles
is darkened and given a bevel, lit consistently from one corner so one wall of
each channel catches light while the opposite wall falls into shadow. That is
what makes the tiles sit proud of the surface rather than looking drawn on.

Shifted is the disordered one. The grid stays perfectly regular, but every tile
looks somewhere other than where it sits, picking a random offset of up to
`maxOffset` and sampling from there. The picture comes back reassembled slightly
out of order. The gaps between tiles get filled with a colour of your choosing,
with the tile's own home pixel, or with the inverse of it.
