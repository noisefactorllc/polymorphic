Stray light bouncing around inside a camera lens: the bright core, a horizontal
streak, an optional star, a rainbow ring, and a chain of coloured blobs marching
across the frame.

Everything is added on top of the picture rather than blended into it, because a
flare is light arriving at the sensor that should not be there, and light only
ever adds.

The chain of blobs is the part that makes it convincing. In a real lens these
are reflections bouncing between glass elements, and they always fall on the
straight line running from the light source through the middle of the frame and
out the other side, spaced by the geometry of the lens. That is what this does
too: each blob sits at a fixed point along that line, with different spacings
for different lens types. Move the light and the whole chain swings around the
centre as one, which is what reads as optics rather than decoration.
