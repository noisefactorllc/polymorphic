Leans the picture over, turning a rectangle into a parallelogram.

It is one line: each row is slid sideways by an amount proportional to how far
up the frame it sits. The top and bottom move in opposite directions and the
middle stays put.

`rotation` is applied first, so the shear happens along a turned axis rather
than the screen's, which lets the two compose into any slant you like rather
than only a horizontal one.
