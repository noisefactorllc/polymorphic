Symmetrical rosettes, the kind you find in stained glass or a kaleidoscope.

The symmetry is not drawn many times over. The effect draws one wedge and folds
the rest of the circle onto it, so a twelve-fold mandala costs the same as a
one-fold one. Every pixel works out its angle around the centre, then that angle
is wrapped into a single slice and mirrored. Whatever gets drawn in the slice
therefore appears identically all the way round, and the symmetry is exact
rather than approximately matched up.

`symmetry` sets how many slices there are, and `layers` stacks concentric rings
of them outward from the middle. `shape` picks what sits in each ring, a petal,
a triangle, or a simple dot. `twist` is the one to reach for: it rotates each
ring a little further than the one inside it, which turns a set of rings into a
spiral. `bindu` adds the dot at the very centre.
