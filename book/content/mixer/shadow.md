Casts a shadow from one picture onto the other.

The shape is cut down to a hard silhouette first, then offset and blurred, and
that blurred silhouette is laid over the base in whatever colour you choose.

Cutting to a silhouette before blurring is what makes it a shadow rather than a
blurred copy. A shadow has no detail inside it. It is the outline of the thing
blocking the light and nothing else, so any internal texture has to go before
the blur, not after.

Pick a bright colour instead of a dark one and the same machinery becomes a
glow, because the geometry of a glow and a drop shadow are identical. Only the
way it combines with the picture differs.
